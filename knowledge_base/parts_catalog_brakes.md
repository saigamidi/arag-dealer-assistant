## Installation Notes
All brake rotor replacements require torque spec verification per the service manual (standard torque: 90–100 ft-lbs for lug nuts). Brake pad sets include anti-squeal shims as standard.

## Warranty
Brake components carry a 12-month/12,000-mile parts warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear is not covered.
