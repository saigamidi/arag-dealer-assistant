# Standard Vehicle Warranty Policy

All new vehicles sold through authorized dealers are covered under the Standard Limited Warranty for **3 years or 60,000 miles**, whichever occurs first. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for all factory-installed components.

## Coverage Includes
- Engine and transmission components
- Electrical systems (factory-installed)
- Air conditioning and heating systems
- Factory-installed infotainment systems

## Coverage Excludes
- Tires (covered separately under manufacturer tire warranty)
- Wear items: brake pads, wiper blades, light bulbs
- Damage from accidents, misuse, or unauthorized modifications
- Routine maintenance items (oil changes, filter replacements)

## Powertrain Extended Warranty
The powertrain (engine, transmission, drivetrain) is covered for an additional period of **5 years or 100,000 miles** under the Extended Powertrain Warranty, automatically included at no extra cost.

## Claim Process
Dealers must submit a warranty claim through the internal claims portal within 30 days of the repair date. Claims require: VIN, odometer reading, description of defect, and diagnostic report. Claims are typically reviewed within 5 business days.

## Transferability
The warranty is transferable to a second owner if the vehicle is sold privately within the original coverage period. No transfer fee applies.
