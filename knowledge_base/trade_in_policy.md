# Vehicle Trade-In Valuation Policy

## Valuation Process
Trade-in vehicles are appraised using the internal valuation tool, which factors in mileage, condition, market demand, and accident history (via vehicle history report). Appraisals are valid for 7 days.

## Condition Categories
| Category | Description | Typical Value Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | No visible wear, full service history | Base valuation |
| Good | Minor cosmetic wear, routine maintenance | -5% to -8% |
| Fair | Noticeable wear, some deferred maintenance | -12% to -18% |
| Poor | Mechanical issues or significant damage | -25% or more, case-by-case |

## Outstanding Loan Balances
If the trade-in vehicle has an outstanding loan balance exceeding the appraised value (negative equity), the difference may be rolled into the new vehicle financing, subject to lender approval.

## Required Documentation
- Vehicle title or current registration
- Valid ID matching the title holder
- Loan payoff statement (if applicable)

## Dealer Discretion
Dealers may offer up to 10% above the system-generated appraisal as a competitive incentive, logged with justification in the DMS trade-in module.
