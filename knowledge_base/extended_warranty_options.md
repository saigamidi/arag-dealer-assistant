# Extended Warranty Purchase Options

Dealers may offer customers optional extended warranty plans beyond the standard coverage period.

## Available Plans

| Plan Name | Duration | Mileage Limit | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential Plus | +2 years | +40,000 miles | $899 |
| Comprehensive | +4 years | +80,000 miles | $1,599 |
| Premium Care | +6 years | +120,000 miles | $2,299 |

## Eligibility
Extended warranties must be purchased within 90 days of the original vehicle sale date. Vehicles older than 1 year or with more than 15,000 miles at time of purchase are not eligible.

## What's Covered
All Premium Care and Comprehensive plans cover the same components as the Standard Warranty, plus:
- Navigation and infotainment electronics
- Hybrid/EV battery systems (Premium Care only)
- Roadside assistance for the duration of the plan

## Cancellation
Customers may cancel an extended warranty within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, provided no claims have been filed. After 30 days, refunds are prorated based on remaining coverage.

## Dealer Commission
Dealers receive a 12% commission on each extended warranty plan sold, paid out in the following month's settlement cycle.
