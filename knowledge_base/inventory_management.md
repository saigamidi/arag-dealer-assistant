# Dealer Inventory Management Guidelines

## Inventory Allocation
Vehicle inventory is allocated monthly based on a dealer's trailing 90-day sales velocity, regional demand forecasts, and facility capacity. Allocation requests can be adjusted via the Inventory Planning tool.

## Minimum Stocking Requirements
Dealers are required to maintain a minimum of 10 units per core model line to remain in good standing for allocation priority. Falling below this threshold for 60 consecutive days triggers a review.

## Inter-Dealer Transfers
Dealers may request a transfer of a specific vehicle from another dealer's lot through the Inter-Dealer Trade system. Transfers require mutual agreement and typically incur a $150 logistics fee, split evenly unless otherwise negotiated.

## Aged Inventory
Vehicles on a dealer's lot for more than 120 days are flagged as "aged inventory" and become eligible for manufacturer-supported clearance pricing, reducing dealer floorplan interest costs.

## Floorplan Financing
Dealers finance inventory through floorplan credit lines, with interest accruing daily until the vehicle is sold. Interest rates are reviewed quarterly based on the prime rate plus an agreed margin.
