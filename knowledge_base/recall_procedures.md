# Vehicle Recall Procedures

## Recall Notification
When a recall is issued, affected dealers are notified via the Service Portal within 24 hours, with a list of affected VINs in their sales/service territory. Customers are notified by mail per regulatory requirements within 5 business days.

## Dealer Responsibilities
1. Check incoming service vehicles against the active recall list before any other work is performed.
2. Perform recall remedies at no cost to the customer, regardless of warranty status.
3. Submit recall completion records through the Warranty Claims system within 48 hours of completion.

## Parts Availability for Recalls
Recall-related parts are prioritized in the supply chain and typically ship within 3–5 business days of a claim being logged, faster than standard parts orders.

## Customer Communication
If a required part is on backorder, dealers must log a temporary measure (if applicable, e.g., a safety advisory) and provide the customer an estimated remedy date, updated weekly until resolved.

## Reporting
Dealers must report any safety-related complaint potentially linked to a known or suspected defect within 24 hours via the Safety Issues hotline, separate from standard warranty claims.
