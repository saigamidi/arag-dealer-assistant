# Customer Complaint Handling Process

## Complaint Channels
Customers may raise complaints through the dealer directly, the manufacturer's customer care line, or the online feedback portal. All channels feed into the centralized Customer Relations Management (CRM) system.

## Response Time Standards
| Severity | Initial Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical (safety-related) | Within 4 hours | Within 48 hours |
| High (vehicle non-operational) | Within 24 hours | Within 5 business days |
| Standard (service/billing dispute) | Within 48 hours | Within 10 business days |

## Escalation Path
If a dealer cannot resolve a complaint within the target window, it is automatically escalated to the Regional Customer Relations team. Unresolved escalations beyond 15 business days are reviewed by the National Customer Experience team.

## Goodwill Adjustments
Dealers may offer goodwill gestures (service discounts, loaner extensions) up to $300 in value without approval. Larger goodwill requests require regional manager sign-off, submitted via the Goodwill Request form.

## Documentation
All complaint interactions must be logged in the CRM with timestamps, including phone calls and in-person conversations, to maintain an auditable resolution history.
