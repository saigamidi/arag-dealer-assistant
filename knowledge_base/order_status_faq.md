# Order Status & Tracking FAQ

## How is order status determined?
Orders move through four stages: **Placed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered**. Status updates are generated automatically from the logistics system and typically refresh every 4 hours.

## Note on Real-Time Lookups
This document provides general guidance only. For the **current status of a specific order**, dealers should query the live Order Status system directly using the order ID, as statuses change frequently and are not reflected in static documentation.

## Typical Processing Times
| Order Type | Typical Processing Time |
|---|---|
| In-stock parts | 1–2 business days |
| Special-order parts | 10–15 business days |
| Vehicle factory orders | 6–10 weeks |

## Delayed Orders
Orders exceeding their expected timeframe by more than 5 business days are automatically flagged for review by the logistics team, and dealers receive a proactive notification with a revised estimate.

## Order Cancellation
Orders may be cancelled free of charge before they reach "Processing" status. Once an order enters "Processing," a cancellation fee of 10% of order value may apply for special-order items.
