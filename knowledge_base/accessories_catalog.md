## Installation
Most accessories can be installed at time of delivery (pre-delivery inspection) at no additional labor charge. Rear seat entertainment packages require a dedicated 2-hour installation slot booked separately.

## Return Eligibility
Accessories follow the standard Return Policy: unopened items returnable within 30 days. Installed accessories are non-returnable but covered under a 12-month installation warranty.
