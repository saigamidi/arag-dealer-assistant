# Service Appointment Scheduling Policy

## Booking Windows
Service appointments can be booked up to 60 days in advance through the Dealer Service Portal or by phone. Same-day appointments are subject to bay availability and are not guaranteed.

## Standard Service Slot Durations
| Service Type | Standard Duration |
|---|---|
| Oil change & inspection | 45 minutes |
| Brake pad replacement | 90 minutes |
| Tire rotation | 30 minutes |
| Full diagnostic scan | 60 minutes |
| Major service (60k/100k mile) | 3–4 hours |

## Cancellation & Rescheduling
Customers may cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before the appointment without penalty. Same-day cancellations after the 2-hour window may incur a $25 no-show fee, at dealer discretion.

## Loaner Vehicle Policy
Loaner vehicles are available for services expected to exceed 4 hours, subject to availability. Customers must present a valid driver's license and proof of insurance.

## Priority Scheduling
Customers with an active extended warranty plan (see Extended Warranty Options) receive priority scheduling, with guaranteed appointment slots within 48 hours of request.
