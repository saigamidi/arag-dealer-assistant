# New Dealer Onboarding Process

## Step 1: Application & Approval
Prospective dealers submit an application through the Regional Partner Portal, including business license, facility photos, and projected sales volume. Approval typically takes 3–4 weeks.

## Step 2: Systems Access
Once approved, dealers receive credentials for:
- **Dealer Management System (DMS)** — inventory, sales, and customer records
- **Parts Ordering Portal** — for ordering parts and accessories
- **Service Portal** — for scheduling and warranty claims

## Step 3: Training Requirements
All dealer staff must complete the mandatory 2-day onboarding training covering:
- Sales and financing procedures
- Warranty claim submission
- Customer service standards

Sales staff must additionally complete product certification training for each vehicle line they sell, renewed annually.

## Step 4: Initial Inventory Allocation
New dealers receive an initial inventory allocation based on projected volume, typically 15–25 vehicles across core model lines. Allocation is reviewed quarterly based on sales performance.

## Ongoing Compliance
Dealers are audited annually for facility standards, customer satisfaction scores, and compliance with brand presentation guidelines. Dealers falling below a 3.5/5.0 customer satisfaction average for two consecutive quarters enter a formal improvement plan.
