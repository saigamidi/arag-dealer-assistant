# Inventory Lookup FAQ

## How do I check current stock for a part?
General stocking patterns and reorder thresholds are described below. For the **exact current quantity** of a specific part at a specific warehouse, dealers must query the live Inventory system, since stock levels change continuously throughout the day and are not captured in static reference material.

## Standard Reorder Thresholds
Parts are automatically reordered when stock falls below the following thresholds:
- High-velocity parts (filters, fluids): reorder at 20% of max stock
- Medium-velocity parts (brakes, batteries): reorder at 30% of max stock
- Low-velocity parts (special-order electrical): manual reorder only

## Warehouse Network
The distribution network consists of 4 regional warehouses. Standard shipping time from warehouse to dealer is 1–3 business days depending on region.

## Backorder Handling
If a part is out of stock across all warehouses, it is marked as backordered and dealers receive an estimated restock date, typically updated weekly until the part is available again.
