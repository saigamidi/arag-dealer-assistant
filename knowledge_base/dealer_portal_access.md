# Dealer Portal Access & Permissions

## User Roles
| Role | Access Level |
|---|---|
| Sales Associate | Inventory viewing, customer records (read-only for pricing) |
| Service Advisor | Service scheduling, warranty claims, parts ordering |
| Dealer Principal | Full access, including financial reports and staff management |
| Regional Admin | Cross-dealer visibility within assigned region |

## Password & Security Policy
Portal passwords must be reset every 90 days and meet minimum complexity requirements (12+ characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols). Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all Dealer Principal and Regional Admin accounts.

## Account Lockout
Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Unlock requests must go through the IT Service Desk with identity verification.

## Access Revocation
When staff leave a dealership, access must be revoked within 24 hours by the Dealer Principal or Regional Admin. Failure to revoke access in a timely manner may affect the dealership's compliance audit score.

## Support Contact
Technical issues with the portal should be logged through the IT Service Desk ticketing system, with a standard response SLA of 4 business hours.
