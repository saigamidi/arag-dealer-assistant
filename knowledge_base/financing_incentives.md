# Customer Financing & Incentive Programs

## Standard Financing
Customers may finance through the manufacturer's preferred lending partner at rates starting from 3.9% APR for qualified buyers (credit score 700+), with terms from 24 to 72 months.

## Current Incentive Programs
| Program | Details | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| First-Time Buyer Credit | $1,000 off MSRP | Ongoing |
| Military & Veteran Discount | $750 off MSRP | Ongoing |
| Loyalty Trade-In Bonus | $500 additional trade-in value for existing owners | Reviewed quarterly |
| Seasonal Clearance | Up to $2,000 off select 2024 model-year inventory | Ends when inventory depleted |

## Lease Options
Lease terms are available on select models (Sedan EX, SUV Touring) with terms of 24 or 36 months. Standard mileage allowance is 12,000 miles/year, with a $0.20/mile excess fee.

## Dealer-Administered Discounts
Dealers may apply discretionary discounts up to 5% of MSRP without regional approval. Discounts exceeding 5% require regional sales manager sign-off, logged in the DMS.

## Documentation Required for Financing
Proof of income, valid ID, and proof of residence are required for all financing applications. Pre-approval decisions are typically returned within 15 minutes through the lending partner's portal.
