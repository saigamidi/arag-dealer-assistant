# Vehicle Return and Exchange Policy

## New Vehicle Returns
New vehicles may be returned within **5 calendar days** of delivery, provided the vehicle has fewer than 250 miles on the odometer and shows no signs of damage or modification. A restocking fee of $500 applies to all approved returns.

## Parts Returns
Unused, unopened parts may be returned within **30 days** of purchase with original receipt and packaging for a full refund. Electrical components and special-order parts are **non-returnable** once the packaging has been opened.

## Exchange Process
1. Dealer submits a return request through the Dealer Portal under "Returns & Exchanges."
2. Regional manager approval is required for any return involving a vehicle with more than 100 miles.
3. Approved returns are processed within 7–10 business days, with funds credited to the dealer's account.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Custom or special-order parts
- Floor mats, seat covers, or installed accessories
- Any item marked "Final Sale" in the catalog

## Damaged or Defective Items
If a part arrives damaged or defective, dealers should file a claim within 48 hours of receipt via the Quality Issues form, including photos of the damage. Replacement parts are shipped at no additional cost once approved.
