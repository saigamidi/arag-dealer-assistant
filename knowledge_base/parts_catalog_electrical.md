Infotainment control modules (ELC-3030) are special-order items with a typical lead time of 10–15 business days. These are **non-returnable** once installed or packaging is opened, per the Return Policy.

## Diagnostic Requirement
Alternator and control module replacements require a diagnostic trouble code (DTC) report attached to the parts order for warranty eligibility.
